Family Matters

Family Law & Domestic Legal Solutions

Family legal matters require discretion, empathy, and practical guidance. We provide clear legal support for divorce, parental responsibilities and rights, care and contact arrangements, and maintenance disputes.

Are you dealing with any of these family legal matters?

Contested or uncontested divorce and division of matrimonial property
Care and contact arrangements and parenting plans
Parental Responsibilities and Rights agreements for mothers and fathers
Child and spousal maintenance applications or enforcement
Protection orders in domestic violence or harassment matters
Cohabitation agreements and legal advice on universal partnerships

What We Cover

Supportive, Practical Family Counsel

We approach every family matter with care while ensuring your rights and your children's best interests are safeguarded. Where possible, we facilitate constructive settlement negotiations to minimize conflict.

Divorce Proceedings

Guiding clients through contested and uncontested divorces, settlement agreements, and equitable asset division.

Care & Contact Arrangements

Drafting practical parenting plans and formal agreements regarding primary residence, contact schedules, and holiday arrangements.

Parental Responsibilities & Rights

Advising and drafting agreements for mothers and fathers regarding guardianship, decision-making, and full parental rights under the Children's Act.

Maintenance Matters

Handling child and spousal maintenance applications, variations, and enforcement through the Maintenance Court.

Protection Orders

Urgent assistance with domestic violence protection orders and harassment orders to ensure personal safety and legal protection.

Cohabitation Agreements

Drafting domestic partnership and cohabitation agreements to provide legal certainty for unmarried couples.
